# EMS (Employee Management System) — Admin User Guide

> This guide is written for non-technical users. No IT knowledge is required. It covers everything an Admin can see and do inside the system.

---

## What Is This System?

EMS is an online platform your company uses to manage everything related to employees — from the moment someone is hired to the day they leave. As an Admin, you have the highest level of access and can control all parts of the system.

You log in through a web browser. No software installation is needed.

---

## How to Log In

1. Open your web browser and go to your company's EMS link.
2. On the login page, enter your **email address** and **password**.
3. Click **Login**.
4. If you forget your password, click **"Forgot Password"** and follow the steps sent to your email.

---

## Your Dashboard

After logging in, you land on the **Admin Dashboard**. Think of it as your home screen. It shows you a quick summary of:

- How many employees are currently active
- Who checked in to work today
- Pending leave requests waiting for approval
- Upcoming payroll deadlines
- Recent notifications and alerts

You can navigate to any section using the menu on the left side of the screen.

---

## Section 1 — Employee Management

**What it does:** This is where you manage all employee records.

### What you can do:
- **Add a new employee** — Fill in their name, job title, department, start date, contact details, and assign them a role.
- **View all employees** — See a list of everyone in the company with their status (active, on leave, terminated).
- **Open an employee's profile** — Click on any employee to see their full details: personal info, work history, documents, attendance, payroll, and more.
- **Update employee information** — Change their department, job title, manager, or any other detail.
- **Terminate an employee** — Mark an employee as no longer active when they leave the company.
- **Promote someone to HR or Manager** — Give an employee elevated access directly from their profile.

---

## Section 2 — Roles & Permissions

**What it does:** Controls who can see and do what inside the system.

### What you can do:
- **Create custom roles** — For example, create a "Finance Manager" role with access only to payroll sections.
- **Assign roles to employees** — Give each person the right level of access.
- **View the role hierarchy** — See how roles are structured from Admin down to Employee.
- **Manage platform roles** — Control system-level roles like HR, Manager, Finance, and Executive.

> Think of roles like keys — each role unlocks certain doors in the system.

---

## Section 3 — Organization Settings

**What it does:** Manage your company's profile and how the system is set up.

### What you can do:
- **Update company profile** — Company name, logo, address, and contact details.
- **Set up departments** — Add or edit the departments in your company (e.g., Sales, Engineering, HR).
- **Manage job titles/designations** — Add the job titles used in your company.
- **Add office locations/branches** — If your company has multiple offices, add them here. This is also used for location-based attendance tracking.
- **Set working hours and shift types** — Define what a standard workday looks like.
- **Manage your subscription plan** — View or upgrade your EMS subscription.
- **Set your company's timezone and language** — Ensure dates and times show correctly for your region.

---

## Section 4 — Attendance

**What it does:** Track when employees come to work and when they leave.

### What you can do:
- **View today's attendance** — See who has checked in, who is on break, and who hasn't arrived yet.
- **View attendance history** — Look back at any employee's attendance for any date range.
- **Set attendance rules** — Define rules like: employees must check in from the office location (geo-fencing), or within a certain time window.
- **Export attendance reports** — Download attendance data as a PDF.
- **Review attendance regularization requests** — When an employee missed a check-in by mistake, they can submit a correction request. You approve or reject it here.

---

## Section 5 — Leave Management

**What it does:** Handle all employee leave — from applying to approving.

### What you can do:
- **View all leave requests** — See every pending, approved, or rejected leave request across the company.
- **Approve or reject leave** — Review requests and take action.
- **Set leave policies** — Define how many days of each leave type (sick, casual, annual) employees get per year.
- **View leave balances** — Check how many leave days any employee has remaining.
- **Manage compensatory off** — If an employee worked on a holiday, you can grant them a compensatory day off.
- **Leave encashment** — Process requests where employees want to convert unused leave into payment.
- **Leave reports** — Generate reports showing leave trends across the company.

## Section 6 — Work From Home (WFH)

## Section 8 — Payroll

**What it does:** Calculate and process employee salaries.

### What you can do:
- **Create payroll** — Generate salary records for one employee or all employees for a given month.
- **Process payroll** — Finalize the payroll for a month, making it ready for payment.
- **Disburse salaries** — Mark salaries as paid.
- **Void or correct payroll** — If a mistake was made, you can void a payroll entry and create a corrected one.
- **Download payslips as PDF** — Generate and download payslips for any employee.
- **View payroll reports** — See a summary of total salary costs by month, department, or employee.

---

## Section 9 — Salary Structure

**What it does:** Define how salaries are broken down (basic pay, allowances, deductions, etc.).

### What you can do:
- **Create salary structures** — Define templates like "Junior Engineer Structure" with specific components (Basic, HRA, PF deduction, etc.).
- **Apply a structure to an employee** — Assign the right salary structure to each employee.
- **Calculate salary components** — The system automatically calculates each component based on the structure.
- **Generate payroll from structure** — Once a structure is applied, payroll can be generated automatically.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I undo a payroll that was processed by mistake?**
Yes. Go to Payroll, find the entry, and use the "Void" option. You can then create a corrected payroll.

**Q: How do I give an employee access to the system?**
When you add an employee, they automatically get an Employee account. To give them more access (e.g., Manager or HR), go to their profile and change their role.

**Q: Can employees see each other's salaries?**
No. Employees can only see their own payslips. Only Admin, HR, and Finance roles can view payroll data.

**Q: What happens when I terminate an employee?**
Their account is deactivated. They can no longer log in. Their records remain in the system for historical reference.

**Q: How do I reset an employee's password?**
Go to the employee's profile and use the password reset option, or ask the employee to use "Forgot Password" on the login page.

**Q: Can I export data?**
Yes. Most sections have an export option (PDF or data download). Reports can also be generated and downloaded.
